1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how CNTXT uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on app.munsit.com and any related pages, features, or services that link to this policy.

2. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device when the user visits a website. They help a website recognise a browser or device, remember settings, keep users signed in, measure usage, and support advertising or attribution functions.

In addition to cookies, websites may use similar technologies such as pixels, tags, web beacons, SDKs, and local storage. A compliant cookie disclosure typically covers these technologies together because they can be used for analytics, advertising, and service functionality in similar ways.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where applicable law requires prior consent, non-essential cookies and similar technologies are used only after the user has given consent through the cookie banner or preference centre. Essential cookies do not require consent because they are necessary for the operation and security of the Website. When a user first visits the Website, the user may accept or decline non-essential cookies. Consent choices may later be changed by using the "Cookie Settings" link in the website footer; withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Third-party cookies

Some cookies and similar technologies on the Website are placed by third-party service providers. Based on the current implementation, these providers may include, amongst others, Meta (Facebook), Google, MoEngage, HubSpot, and Adjust.

These providers may collect information through their own cookies or tracking tools and may use that information for their own purposes in accordance with their own privacy notices and terms.

6. California privacy notice

If a user is a California resident, the user may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, including where cookies or other tracking technologies are used for those purposes. California residents may exercise this choice by clicking the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link made available on the Website, where applicable.
